## SCENE 1 — HOOK
- **VO:** "Why did the monk behind Journey to the West need seventeen years to bring books back from India, just beyond the Himalayas? The answer is on this route, and in one emperor's ban."
- **Hình:** Toàn cảnh châu Á: Trường An → dãy Himalaya → Ấn Độ; "17 YEARS" từ frame 0; cả tuyến đi + về loé lên trong câu hook (RoutePreview). Dòng nguồn "According to his disciple Huili's biography". Tô sáng dãy Himalaya ở chữ "Himalayas".

## SCENE 2 — RA ĐI — LỆNH CẤM
- **VO:** "Chang'an, China's capital. A young monk, Xuanzang, wants the original Buddhist texts of India. But the emperor has closed the borders. No one may leave. So he slips past the frontier watchtowers at night."
- **Hình:** Trường An (Tây An): chân dung Huyền Trang cõng gùi kinh (thời Kamakura, PD). Biên giới nhà Đường tô đỏ mờ ở "closed the borders". Đêm: các tháp canh (ghim nhỏ), tuyến lẻn qua.

## SCENE 3 — NHỊP LẶNG — SA MẠC
- **VO:** "His guide turns back. He crosses the desert alone, on one old horse. His water bag slips from his hands. Four nights and five days, without a drop of water. He turns toward China, then stops. Better to die going west, he said, than to live by returning east."
- **Hình:** Sa mạc Mạc Hạ Diên (Gobi): camera đẩy rất chậm, không xoay; ảnh AI toàn khung `full-desert.jpg` (nhà sư và con ngựa đỏ gầy giữa sa mạc); "4 NIGHTS · 5 DAYS"; chỉ gió sa mạc, nhạc hạ, không tiếng sự kiện.

## SCENE 4 — TURPAN · VÒNG QUA HIMALAYA
- **VO:** "At Turpan, a king tries to keep him at his court. Xuanzang refuses to eat for three days, until the king lets him go. The straight road south is blocked by the Himalayas, so he goes around them. Seven days over the glaciers of the Tian Shan. More than a dozen of his party die of cold and hunger. Then Samarkand, Bamiyan, and over the Hindu Kush into India."
- **Hình:** Cao Xương (Turpan) → Kucha → đèo Bedel / Thiên Sơn ("7 DAYS", gió lạnh, ảnh AI toàn khung `full-glacier.jpg`) → Issyk-Kul → Samarkand → Bamiyan → Hindu Kush. Tô sáng dãy Himalaya (vật chắn) khi đọc "blocked by the Himalayas", tuyến vòng lên phía bắc.

## SCENE 5 — BƯỚC NGOẶT — Ở LẠI ẤN ĐỘ
- **VO:** "And here is the twist. He doesn't just collect the books and leave. He stays more than ten years, studying at Nalanda and travelling across India."
- **Hình:** Kéo ra cả Ấn Độ; Nalanda; tuyến đi vòng Ấn Độ vẽ nhanh; "10+ YEARS".

## SCENE 6 — ĐƯỜNG VỀ
- **VO:** "Going home, his boat nearly sinks on the Indus. Fifty manuscripts are lost. From Khotan he writes to the emperor: "I have passed seventeen years." The emperor writes back, overjoyed. He enters Chang'an with six hundred and fifty-seven books. They fill twenty horses."
- **Hình:** Sông Ấn (sông phát sáng T7, thuyền chao; "50 MANUSCRIPTS") → Pamir → Khotan (đường phía nam Taklamakan) → Trường An; "657 BOOKS" · "20 HORSES".

## SCENE 7 — VÌ SAO QUAN TRỌNG
- **VO:** "A pagoda was built to hold them. It still stands in Xi'an. Nine centuries later, his journey became a novel, Journey to the West, with the Monkey King at his side."
- **Hình:** Tây An: Đại Nhạn Tháp (ảnh PD / CC, kiểm giấy phép); tranh minh hoạ Tây Du Ký cổ (PD).

## SCENE 8 — TRẢ GIÁ
- **VO:** "Twenty-five hundred kilometres became seventeen years. He crossed the desert with one horse. He came home with twenty. The monk who fled the empire came home its hero."
- **Hình:** Toàn tuyến sáng; "2,500 KM" → "17 YEARS"; "1 HORSE → 20"; câu cuối đẩy về Trường An.
