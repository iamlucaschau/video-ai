## SCENE 2 — RA ĐI

## SCENE 3 — BEECHEY
- **VO:** "They spent their first winter in the harbour of Beechey Island. Three men died there. Searchers later found their graves, but no note saying where the ships went."

## SCENE 4 — KẸT BĂNG
- **VO:** "In 1846 they sailed south, and took the west side of King William Island. It was the wrong side. The ice there doesn't always melt in summer. In September, the ice closed around both ships, and held them for nineteen months. In May 1847, officers left a note on the shore. It said: "All well." They were less than 150 kilometres from waters already on the map."
- **Hình:** Eo Peel → bờ tây đảo King William; bờ đông mờ (lối Amundsen). Tô dòng băng phía tây (đỏ). Ảnh AI toàn khung `full-ice.jpg` ở "the ice closed"; chip "19 MONTHS". Victory Point: chữ vàng "ALL WELL"; đường thẳng tới bờ biển đã biết, "< 150 km".

## SCENE 5 — NHỊP LẶNG — TỜ GIẤY
- **VO:** "A year later, someone wrote around the edges of that same note. Twenty-four men were dead, Franklin among them. The other 105 had left the ships, and would walk south. Then the note went back under the stones."

## SCENE 6 — BƯỚC NGOẶT — NGƯỜI INUIT
- **VO:** "And here is the twist. In 1854, Inuit told John Rae what they knew: men dragging a boat south, starving. In London, the story was attacked. Charles Dickens himself wrote against it. One search ship, Resolute, was abandoned in the ice. Its timber became the desk in the Oval Office. The note was found in 1859, under those stones. But the ships stayed lost."
- **Hình:** "THE TWIST"; vịnh Pelly: thẻ chân dung John Rae (PD); ảnh AI `full-march.jpg` ở "men dragging a boat south"; London (bay xa): "ATTACKED" đỏ; vị trí *Resolute* bị bỏ: thẻ ảnh bàn Resolute (ảnh Nhà Trắng, PD); Victory Point "1859"; hai dấu hỏi trên biển.

## SCENE 7 — TÌM THẤY
