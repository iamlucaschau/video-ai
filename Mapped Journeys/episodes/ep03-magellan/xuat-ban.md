> Magellan set out in 1519 to find a western shortcut to the Spice Islands. He expected the sea beyond South America to take three or four days. It took ninety-eight, and the whole voyage nearly three years. The maps of his time made the world too small, the short way east around Africa belonged to Portugal by treaty, and the Pacific turned out to be the largest ocean on Earth. Five ships left Spain; one came home, with eighteen men aboard. A map, a treaty, or the ocean itself: which one really cost him three years?
