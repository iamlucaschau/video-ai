## Mô tả

> In 2007, by his own count, Terrance Watanabe bet more than $825 million in Las Vegas and lost nearly $127 million, about 5.6% of the casino company's Las Vegas gambling revenue that year. The Wynn banned him over his drinking. Caesars Palace and the Rio gave him a bellman and a security guard of his own. When $14.7 million went unpaid, the casino took him to the district attorney. Years later, New Jersey fined Caesars $225,000 over how it handled him. So when a casino's best customer can't stand up, who is supposed to say stop?
