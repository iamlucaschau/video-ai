# EPISODE SPEC: slot-hack (NEON ODDS, style N, 9:16, 30 fps)

## SCENE 1 · HOOK
- **VO:** "Imagine a phone that tells you the exact moment to hit spin on a slot machine."

## SCENE 2 · BẬC 1 · ST. LOUIS
- **VO:** "In June twenty fourteen, a man in a St. Louis casino had one. He held it close to the screen. His finger hovered over the button, then jabbed it. He won just over twenty-one thousand dollars. The casino's machines had paid out more than they took in, with no big jackpot. And nobody had touched them."

## SCENE 3 · BẬC 2 · NƯỚC NGA
- **VO:** "The trail led to Russia. In twenty oh nine, Russia banned almost all gambling. Casinos had to sell thousands of slot machines cheap. Some went to a team in St. Petersburg. Not to play. To study the code."

## SCENE 4 · BẬC 3 · CÔNG THỨC
- **VO:** "Every slot machine runs on a random number generator. But it isn't truly random. It's a formula, fed by the machine's clock. Learn it, and you only need to find the machine's place in it."

## SCENE 5 · BẬC 4 · CÚ RUNG
- **VO:** "So an agent films about two dozen spins and sends the video to St. Petersburg. The team works out the pattern. Then the phone buzzes, about a quarter of a second early, because that's how long a human needs to react. He doesn't choose when to spin. The phone does."

## SCENE 6 · BẬC 5 · TIỀN
- **VO:** "Agents kept each machine under a thousand dollars, so nobody would notice. One security expert says an agent usually won over ten thousand dollars a day. A small team? More than a quarter of a million a week. Ninety percent went back to St. Petersburg."

## SCENE 7 · BẬC 6 · BẮT
- **VO:** "That December, the man from St. Louis came back. He and three others were arrested in Missouri. The Russians each got two years in prison. But the man behind it, a mathematician known only as Alex, stayed in Russia."

## SCENE 8 · CÚ LẬT
- **VO:** "So casinos threw those machines out, right? No. There was no easy fix, and keeping them was cheaper than replacing them. Alex even offered to sell the maker the fix. His price ran to eight figures. The company said no."

## SCENE 9 · KẾT
- **VO:** "Years later, more than a hundred thousand of that model were still on casino floors. So when a finger waits over the spin button, is it waiting for luck, or for a phone?"
