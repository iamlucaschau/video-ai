# EPISODE SPEC: ron-harris (NEON ODDS, style N, 9:16, 30 fps)

## SCENE 1 · HOOK
- **VO:** "What if the man who checks the slot machines is the one rigging them?"

## SCENE 2 · BẬC 1 · NGƯỜI KIỂM TRA
- **VO:** "In the early nineties, Ron Harris was a computer expert at Nevada's Gaming Control Board. His job was to find cheating software hidden in casino games."

## SCENE 3 · BẬC 2 · CHIẾC LAPTOP
- **VO:** "Agents checked slot machines by plugging in a laptop. Harris wrote the program on those laptops. And he hid something inside it."

## SCENE 4 · BẬC 3 · THỨ TỰ ĐỒNG XU
- **VO:** "Every time an agent tested a machine, the program quietly changed it. Now it paid a jackpot when coins went in a set order. Only Harris and his friends knew the order."

## SCENE 5 · BẬC 4 · TIỀN
- **VO:** "His friends hit jackpots in Las Vegas, Reno and Lake Tahoe. Prosecutors could prove at least forty-two thousand dollars. Nobody knows the real total."

## SCENE 6 · BẬC 5 · KENO
- **VO:** "Then he went further. He built a program that predicted keno numbers without touching the machine at all. In Las Vegas, he and a partner took ten thousand dollars from a keno machine in under five minutes."

## SCENE 7 · BẬC 6 · ATLANTIC CITY
- **VO:** "So in January nineteen ninety-five, they flew to Atlantic City. The partner bought keno tickets at Bally's. One ticket hit: a hundred thousand dollars."

## SCENE 8 · CÚ LẬT
- **VO:** "And that's what sank them. A win that big made the casino suspicious. Before a cent was paid, the partner was arrested. Harris was picked up when his plane landed in Las Vegas. The judge gave him seven years. "The depth of your betrayal," he said, "was complete.""

## SCENE 9 · KẾT
