## SCENE 1 · HOOK
- **VO:** "This man won seven point seven million pounds at a London casino, without touching the cards that won it."

## SCENE 2 · BẬC 1 · VẾT LỖI
- **VO:** "This game is pure chance, and the house keeps about a one percent edge. But the pattern on these cards sat less than a millimeter off center. So one long edge looked slightly different from the other. Alone, that was useless, because every card had the same flaw. Phil Ivey needed the good cards facing one way, and the rest facing the other."

## SCENE 3 · BẬC 2 · ÂN HUỆ
- **VO:** "But at this table, only the dealer touches the cards. So his partner asked her for a small favor. Turn the good ones this way, and the bad ones that way. To change the luck, she said. Casinos hear that all the time. The dealer had no idea why."

## SCENE 4 · BẬC 3 · GIỮ BỘ BÀI
- **VO:** "Then Ivey said he had won with those cards, and wanted to keep them. The casino agreed. A shuffling machine mixed them without turning a single one. Now the back of the next card gave it away. One percent for the house could become six and a half for him."

## SCENE 5 · BẬC 4 · TIỀN
- **VO:** "His bets had started as low as four thousand pounds. Now they hit one hundred and fifty thousand, the casino's limit. In one stretch, he read the next card right seventeen times out of nineteen. In two days, he was up seven point seven million pounds. That same year, the pair took an American casino for nine point six million dollars. That casino paid."

## SCENE 6 · CÚ LẬT

He had fixed the deck, they said, using the dealer as his hands. Clever, skillful, and still cheating."

## SCENE 7 · MỸ
