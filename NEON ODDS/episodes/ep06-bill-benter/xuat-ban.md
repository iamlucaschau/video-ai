## Mô tả

> Bill Benter was blacklisted from Las Vegas blackjack, so he took three computers to Hong Kong and built a model to beat the horse-racing pool. The trick was not ignoring the crowd but correcting its odds. In 2001 one of his 51,000 bets hit a $16 million jackpot, and he never cashed it. So when you bet on a horse, who else is in that pool with you?
