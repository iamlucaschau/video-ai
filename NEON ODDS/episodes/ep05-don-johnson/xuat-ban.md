## Mô tả

> In 2011, Don Johnson won about $15 million at blackjack from three Atlantic City casinos in six months, betting $100,000 a hand. He didn't count cards. During the recession, the casinos competed for high rollers with better rules and a 20% rebate on big losses. So when a casino offers you a deal, who did the math first?
