# EPISODE SPEC: ritz-roulette (NEON ODDS, style N, 9:16, 30 fps)

## SCENE 1 · HOOK
- **VO:** "Would you pay one point three million pounds to a man you were sure had cheated you?"

## SCENE 2 · BẬC 1 · BÀN ROULETTE
- **VO:** "March twenty oh four, the Ritz casino in London. A thin man in rimless glasses sits down at roulette. The wheel is a standard European model: thirty-seven pockets, one green zero. Casinos know him as Niko Tosa. He waits about six seconds, until the ball starts to slow. Then he and a partner lunge in, covering up to fifteen numbers at once."

## SCENE 3 · BẬC 2 · CHUỖI THẮNG
- **VO:** "They hit about as often as they miss. Sometimes thirteen times in a row. Soon they're risking fifteen thousand pounds on one spin. Counting earlier nights, the group is up about one point three million pounds. And he tells staff he'll be back tomorrow."

## SCENE 4 · BẬC 3 · BẮT
- **VO:** "The security chief studies the tapes. Six seconds, he thinks, is enough time for a hidden computer. So the next night, police are waiting. All three are arrested on suspicion of deception. Officers seize four phones and a pocket computer."

## SCENE 5 · BẬC 4 · LASER
- **VO:** "Then the papers get hold of it. A laser hidden in a mobile phone, they say, wired to a tiny computer. The laser scam."

## SCENE 6 · BẬC 5 · THẢ
- **VO:** "But the searches find no earpiece, no wires, no timer. And Britain's gaming law dates back to eighteen forty-five. It says nothing about computers. Police drop the case, and the Ritz has to pay. The three walk away with their winnings."

## SCENE 7 · CÚ LẬT
- **VO:** "Here's the thing. There was no laser. Years later, a reporter found him in Croatia. He says he never used a computer. Just practice, on a wheel at home. The weak spot was the wheel. A slightly tilted wheel drops the ball at almost the same spot, spin after spin. And the Ritz wheel, one expert says, was old and predictable. Within months, the Ritz replaced every wheel."

## SCENE 8 · KẾT
- **VO:** "Casinos have one sure fix: no more bets once the ball is moving. They won't use it. So somewhere tonight, someone is still watching the ball slow down."
