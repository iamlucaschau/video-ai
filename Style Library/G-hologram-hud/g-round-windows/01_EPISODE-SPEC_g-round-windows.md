- **VO:** "Every airliner window has rounded corners. That is not for style."

## SCENE 2 — Comet 1952
- **VO:** "In nineteen fifty-two, the de Havilland Comet became the first jet airliner to carry passengers. It flew high and fast, in a pressurized cabin."

## SCENE 3 — Hai vụ vỡ 1954
- **VO:** "Then, in nineteen fifty-four, two Comets broke apart in the sky, three months apart. Every Comet was grounded."

## SCENE 4 — Bể nước thử áp suất
- **VO:** "Engineers put a whole Comet in a giant water tank. They pumped the cabin up and down, flight after flight. After about three thousand flights' worth of pressure, the cabin split open. The crack started at a corner."

## SCENE 5 — Cơ chế: tập trung ứng suất
- **VO:** "Every flight, the cabin swells like a balloon. A sharp corner piles that stress into one tiny point. Metal bent there again and again gets tired, and cracks. Round corners spread the stress out."
