## VOICEOVER (English)

Every airliner window has rounded corners. That is not for style.

In nineteen fifty-two, the de Havilland Comet became the first jet airliner to carry passengers. It flew high and fast, in a pressurized cabin.

Then, in nineteen fifty-four, two Comets broke apart in the sky, three months apart. Every Comet was grounded.

Engineers put a whole Comet in a giant water tank. They pumped the cabin up and down, flight after flight. After about three thousand flights' worth of pressure, the cabin split open. The crack started at a corner.

Every flight, the cabin swells like a balloon. A sharp corner piles that stress into one tiny point. Metal bent there again and again gets tired, and cracks. Round corners spread the stress out.
