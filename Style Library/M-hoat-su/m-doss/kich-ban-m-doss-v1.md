# Test 2 style M "Hoạt Sử" — Desmond Doss, người lính không cầm súng · v1

## VOICEOVER (English)

This soldier refused to carry a gun, and America gave him its highest medal.

His faith said he must never kill. He could have stayed home. He joined the army anyway.

So he went to war with no rifle, only a medic's bag.

On Guam and in the Philippines, he ran to the wounded under fire. He won two Bronze Stars.

Then came Okinawa, 1945. His unit climbed a cliff four hundred feet high. The enemy hit back hard. About seventy-five men went down. The rest pulled back.

He stayed.

One by one, he dragged the wounded to the edge, and lowered them down on a rope.

Weeks later, a grenade tore into his legs. On the stretcher, he saw another wounded man. So he crawled off and gave him his place. Then he was hit again, and his arm broke. He crawled three hundred yards to the aid station.

So did the army ever make him carry a gun? No. In October 1945, President Truman put the Medal of Honor around his neck.

The man who never fired a shot brought back about seventy-five.
