## VOICEOVER (English)

**[H]**

This soldier refused to carry a gun, and America gave him its highest medal.

**[B]**

In 1942, he was offered a way out of the draft. He said no. But his faith said he must never kill.

So he went to war with no rifle, only a medic's bag.

**[L]**

On Guam and in the Philippines, he ran to the wounded under fire, again and again. So the army gave him two Bronze Stars.

**[T]**

Then came Okinawa. His unit climbed a cliff the soldiers called Hacksaw Ridge. It was four hundred feet high. But Japanese troops hit back hard. About seventy-five men went down, and the rest pulled back.

He stayed.

One by one, he dragged the wounded to the edge, and lowered them down on a rope.

Weeks later, a grenade tore into his legs. On the stretcher, he saw another wounded man. So he crawled off and gave him his place. Then he was hit again, and his arm broke. He crawled three hundred yards to get help.

**[Q]**

So did the army ever make him carry a gun? No. That October, President Truman put the Medal of Honor around his neck. He was the first conscientious objector ever to receive it.

**[K]**

The man who never fired a shot brought back about seventy-five.
