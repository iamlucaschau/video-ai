## VOICEOVER (English)

This British officer fought the Second World War with a sword, a longbow and bagpipes.

His name was Jack Churchill. In 1939, he shot for Britain at the world archery championships.

In 1941, he led a raid on Norway playing the bagpipes.

In 1943, in Italy, he was sent to take a German post above Salerno. He went in with one corporal. They came back with forty-two prisoners. He had lost his sword in the fight, so he walked back into the town to get it.

In 1944, on the island of Brač, a mortar shell killed or wounded everyone but him. He played the bagpipes as the Germans advanced. Then a grenade knocked him out.

The Germans flew him to Berlin. They thought he was related to Winston Churchill. He wasn't.

In the camp, he dug a tunnel and got out. They caught him again.

Many people say he killed a German with his longbow. But he said a truck had crushed his bows.

He survived the war and died in 1996.
