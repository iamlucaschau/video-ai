## VOICEOVER (English)

**[H]**

This British officer fought the Second World War with a sword, a longbow and bagpipes.

**[B]**

Everyone else carried rifles. He'd shot arrows for Britain at the world championships.

So when his commandos raided Norway that December, he went ashore playing the bagpipes.

**[L]**

Two years later, in Italy, he was sent to take a German post above Salerno. He took one corporal. They came back with forty-two prisoners, a mortar crew included.

But he'd lost his sword in the fight. So he walked back into the town to get it.

**[T]**

Then came the island of Brač, off Yugoslavia. A mortar shell killed or wounded everyone but him. So he played the bagpipes as the Germans advanced. The song was "Will Ye No Come Back Again?"

Then a grenade knocked him out.

The Germans flew him to Berlin for questioning, because of his surname. They thought he was related to Winston Churchill. He wasn't.

So they locked him in Sachsenhausen. He tunnelled out, and they caught him again.

**[Q]**

Videos online say that in France, in 1940, he killed a German with that longbow. But Churchill himself said a truck had crushed his bows.

**[K]**

The man who took a sword to a gun war outlived it by fifty years.
