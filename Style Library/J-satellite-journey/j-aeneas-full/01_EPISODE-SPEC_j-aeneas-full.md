# EPISODE SPEC — j-aeneas-full (video đầy đủ style J "Satellite Journey")

*Style J (`Style Library/J-satellite-journey/README.md`). 9:16, 30 fps, tiếng Anh. Lời đọc khớp từng chữ với `kich-ban-j-aeneas-full-FINAL.md` (`node scripts/episode.mjs prep` kiểm tra). Kế hoạch hình chi tiết: FINAL, mục "Kế hoạch hình".*

## SCENE 1 — HOOK
- **VO:** "Why did Aeneas, the hero Rome called its founder, wander for seven years, when Italy was three weeks' sail from Troy? The answer is on this route, and in one goddess's grudge."
- **Hình:** Toàn Địa Trung Hải, đẩy chậm + xoay. Dòng nguồn "According to Virgil's Aeneid" ở giây đầu; "7 YEARS" + "vs. 3 weeks"; đường biển thẳng Troy → Tiber vẽ mờ màu vàng. Câu 2: bay xuống Troy, chữ "ONE GODDESS'S GRUDGE".

## SCENE 2 — HỒI 1 — người mẹ nhầm
- **VO:** "It begins in burning Troy, in modern Turkey. Aeneas carries his old father out on his back. First stop: Thrace, where a bleeding myrtle warns him to leave. On Delos, Apollo's oracle tells him: seek your ancient mother. His father reads it as Crete. Then a plague drives them out. In a dream, the gods correct him. The mother is Italy, where his ancestors came from."
- **Hình:** Troy (thẻ Barocci) → Thrace (Janssens) → Delos (Claude Lorrain) → Crete (Raimondi, dịch bệnh) → kéo ra, vòng sóng vàng ở Italy (phần giấc mơ trong bản khắc Raimondi).

## SCENE 3 — HỒI 2 — đường vòng
- **VO:** "A storm throws them onto the islands of the Harpies. Their curse: one day, you will be hungry enough to eat your tables. At Buthrotum, a prophet warns him: don't take the short way. Scylla and Charybdis guard the strait. So they see Italy for the very first time, and have to sail on. They circle Sicily, past the Cyclops under Mount Etna."
- **Hình:** Strophades (Perrier, Harpy; chữ "EAT YOUR TABLES") → Buthrotum (chữ "NO SHORTCUT", dấu X đỏ ở eo Messina) → chạm bờ Italy ở Castrum Minervae (ảnh toàn khung #1) → vòng đông Sicily, ghim Etna (Carracci, Polyphemus).

## SCENE 4 — NHỊP LẶNG
- **VO:** "At Drepanum, his father dies. The man he carried out of burning Troy never sees Italy."
- **Hình:** Drepanum; camera chỉ đẩy rất chậm, không xoay; thẻ Barocci quay lại và phai sang trắng đen; nhạc hạ, ~2 s im lặng sau câu.

## SCENE 5 — HỒI 3 — cơn giận của Juno
- **VO:** "Now the goddess strikes. Juno loves one city above all, Carthage, and a prophecy says Aeneas' descendants will destroy it. Her storm throws the fleet to Africa, right into Carthage. Queen Dido tells him: this is your seventh summer at sea. He stays, until the gods order him to leave. Dido curses his people: our nations will be enemies forever. That is how Virgil explains why Rome and Carthage later fought three wars. At Cumae, the Sibyl leads him into the underworld, where his father shows him Rome."
- **Hình:** Kéo ra rộng: chữ "JUNO", ghim Carthage loé đỏ → bay xuống châu Phi (Rubens, Quos Ego) → Carthage (Turner, Dido building Carthage; chữ "7TH SUMMER") → lời nguyền (ảnh toàn khung #2) → đường đỏ Carthage ↔ Rome, chữ "3 PUNIC WARS" → bay lên Cumae (Turner, Lake Avernus; chữ "THE UNDERWORLD").

## SCENE 6 — TỚI NƠI
- **VO:** "At last, at the mouth of the Tiber, they use flat bread as plates, and eat them. His son laughs: we're eating our tables. The curse was a joke. They had arrived."
- **Hình:** Cửa Tiber, đẩy chậm (Claude Lorrain, Pallanteum); chữ "THEY ARRIVED".

## SCENE 7 — TRẢ GIÁ
- **VO:** "Three weeks of sailing became seven years and more than three thousand kilometres. A misread oracle, a strait he couldn't take, and a goddess who wanted him lost. And his descendants, Romulus and Remus, would found Rome."
- **Hình:** Kéo ra toàn tuyến đã đi cạnh đường thẳng mờ của hook; "3 WEEKS → 7 YEARS" · "3,000+ KM"; câu cuối đẩy về Rome, vòng sóng vàng, nhãn "ROME".
