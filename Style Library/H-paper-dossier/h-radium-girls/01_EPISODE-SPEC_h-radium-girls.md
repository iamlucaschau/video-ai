# Spec — The Radium Girls (test H Paper Dossier)

## SCENE 1 — Hook: sơn phát sáng an toàn
- **VO:** "They were told the glowing paint was safe enough to put in their mouths."

## SCENE 2 — 1917: vẽ mặt đồng hồ
- **VO:** "In nineteen seventeen, young women in New Jersey painted watch dials with radium, so soldiers could read the time in the dark. To keep a fine tip, they shaped each brush with their lips. Lip, dip, paint."

## SCENE 3 — Radium trong xương
- **VO:** "Radium settles in bone, like calcium. Within a few years, some workers' jaws began to crumble. Some died."

## SCENE 4 — Công ty chối, 1925 bằng chứng
- **VO:** "The company blamed other causes. In nineteen twenty-five, a medical examiner tied the deaths to radium."

## SCENE 5 — 1927 kiện, dàn xếp
- **VO:** "In nineteen twenty-seven, five of the women sued. They settled for ten thousand dollars each, and six hundred a year for life."
