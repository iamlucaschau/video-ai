## VOICEOVER (English)

They were told the glowing paint was safe enough to put in their mouths.

In nineteen seventeen, young women in New Jersey painted watch dials with radium, so soldiers could read the time in the dark. To keep a fine tip, they shaped each brush with their lips. Lip, dip, paint.

Radium settles in bone, like calcium. Within a few years, some workers' jaws began to crumble. Some died.

The company blamed other causes. In nineteen twenty-five, a medical examiner tied the deaths to radium.

In nineteen twenty-seven, five of the women sued. They settled for ten thousand dollars each, and six hundred a year for life.
