## VOICEOVER (English)

In 480 BC, the largest empire the world had yet seen marched on Greece.

Its king, Xerxes, ruled from Egypt to the Indus.

To reach Athens, his army had to squeeze through one gap between the mountains and the sea. Thermopylae. In places, barely wide enough for a single cart.

Waiting there: about seven thousand Greeks, led by a Spartan king and three hundred of his men.
