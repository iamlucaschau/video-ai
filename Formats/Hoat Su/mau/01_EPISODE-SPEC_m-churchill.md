## SCENE 2 — CUNG THỦ, NA UY
- **VO:** "Everyone else carried rifles. He'd shot arrows for Britain at the world championships. So when his commandos raided Norway that December, he went ashore playing the bagpipes."

## SCENE 4 — BRAČ
