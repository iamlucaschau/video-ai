# 01 — EPISODE SPEC: "How Poor vs Rich People Give Discounts" (FINAL)

*Kịch bản lock: kich-ban-give-discounts-FINAL.md. Look: Receipt × Paper (bible §2). Timecode ước lượng @150 từ/phút — đo lại theo VO thật ở Giai đoạn 3–4.*

**Caption:** "Do you give discounts like a poor or rich person?"

---

## SCENE 1 — Title + thesis hook (0:00–0:12)
- **VO:** "A poor person hears "Can you do it for less?" and cuts the price. A rich person hears the same question and sells the discount. Same lawn. Opposite answer."
- **Visual:** dải tiêu đề giấy; POOR (trái) và RICH (phải) trượt vào; giữa là một bãi cỏ giấy + bong bóng giấy "Can you do it for less?" của khách (người phụ, xám); "Same lawn" — bãi cỏ nảy nhẹ
- **Text:** `How Poor vs Rich People Give Discounts` / `POOR` (đỏ) / `RICH` (xanh) / `VERSUS` / bong bóng `Can you do it for less?`

## SCENE 2 — Poor: nói "yes" (0:12–0:32)
- **VO:** "The poor person mows lawns. Sixty dollars a cut, every two weeks. A client asks for twenty percent off. They say yes. Forty-eight dollars a cut. The client is happy. They feel like they saved the account. They didn't save a customer. They paid one to stay."
- **Visual:** POOR đứng cạnh máy cắt cỏ trước nhà khách; thẻ giá `$60` ghim tường → gạch → `$48`; khách cười; POOR cười theo; dải giấy chốt câu đắt
- **Text:** `$60 / cut` → `−20%` → `$48` (đỏ) → `"They paid one to stay."`

## SCENE 3 — Poor: phép tính (0:32–1:16) ★ TEST SCENE
- **VO:** "Here's the math they skip. Every cut costs eighteen dollars. Gas, trimmer line, blades. Six of that is just driving to the street. At sixty, they keep forty-two. At forty-eight, they keep thirty. They cut the price twenty percent. They cut their profit almost thirty. Then the neighbor hears about the deal. Same question. Same yes. Next door, there's no driving, so that lawn costs twelve. Forty-eight minus twelve. Thirty-six. Two lawns on one street. Sixty-six dollars a visit. The only one who got a raise was the customer. They lowered the price. They never asked for anything back."
- **Visual:** máy in hóa đơn "THE POOR WAY" in từng dòng; bảng ghim: bình xăng/dây/lưỡi; **hàng xóm thò đầu qua hàng rào** giơ bong bóng "Me too?"; POOR giơ biển `−29%` khi câu "almost thirty"
- **Text (lần lượt trên hóa đơn):** `COST / CUT $18` (`DRIVING $6`) → `$60 − $18 = $42` → `$48 − $18 = $30` → `PRICE −20%` / `PROFIT −29%` (push-in) → `NEIGHBOR $48 − $12 = $36` → `TOTAL / VISIT $66` (đỏ) → dải giấy `"The only one who got a raise was the customer."` → `"They lowered the price. They never asked for anything back."`

## SCENE 4 — Transition (1:16–1:20)
- **VO:** "Here's what a rich person does with the same question."
- **Visual:** bong bóng "Can you do it for less?" trượt sang; RICH bước vào
- **Text:** `the same question`

## SCENE 5 — Rich ①② (1:20–1:38)
- **VO:** "First, they never give a discount away. They trade it. Second, they name the trade. "Twenty percent off, when two neighbors on your street sign up." If nobody signs, the price stays sixty. The client stops asking for less. They start selling for them."
- **Visual:** nhãn `① Trade it` ; thẻ ghim "điều kiện" có 2 ô trống hình nhà; nhãn `② Name the trade`; khách cầm tờ rơi đi gõ cửa hàng xóm
- **Text:** `① Trade it` → `② "20% off, when 2 neighbors sign up"` → `No sign-ups? Still $60.` → `"They start selling for them."`

## SCENE 6 — Rich ③ (1:38–1:47)
- **VO:** "Third, two neighbors sign up. Full price. Sixty dollars each. Same street. No driving between lawns. Twelve dollars each to cut."
- **Visual:** **2 hàng xóm giơ biển SIGNED** (nút thắt: hàng xóm nửa Poor đòi giảm, nửa Rich trả giá đủ); 3 nhà cạnh nhau trên một con đường
- **Text:** `③ 2 neighbors × $60` → `$12 / lawn (no driving)`

## SCENE 7 — Rich ④ (1:47–2:00)
- **VO:** "Fourth, the math. The client: forty-eight minus eighteen. Thirty. Each neighbor: sixty minus twelve. Forty-eight. Ninety-six for both. One hundred twenty-six dollars a visit. Same street. Almost double."
- **Visual:** hóa đơn "THE RICH WAY" in từng dòng, con dấu PAID; push-in vào `$126`
- **Text:** `CLIENT $48 − $18 = $30` → `NEIGHBORS 2 × ($60 − $12) = $96` → `TOTAL / VISIT $126` (xanh) → `≈ 2× $66`

## SCENE 8 — Closing card (2:00–2:14)
- **VO:** "One person gave a discount and got a neighbor asking for one too. The other sold a discount and got two neighbors at full price. Poor people give discounts. Rich people sell them. Same lawn. Opposite answer."
- **Visual:** 2 cột: POOR + 1 hàng xóm giơ biển `$48`, RICH + 2 hàng xóm giơ biển `$60`; bãi cỏ ở giữa; biển POOR `$66`, RICH `$126`
- **Text:** 2 dải bridge couplet → `Poor people give discounts.` (đỏ) / `Rich people sell them.` (xanh) → `Same lawn. Opposite answer.`
