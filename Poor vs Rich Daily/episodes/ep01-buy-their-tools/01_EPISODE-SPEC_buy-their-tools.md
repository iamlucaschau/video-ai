# 01 — EPISODE SPEC: "How Poor vs Rich People Buy Their Tools" (FINAL)

*Kịch bản lock: kich-ban-buy-their-tools-FINAL.md. Timecode ước lượng @150 từ/phút — Claude đo lại theo VO thật (02_REMOTION-TECH-SPEC) rồi hiệu chỉnh.*

**Caption:** "Do you buy tools like a poor or rich person?"

---

## SCENE 1 — Title + thesis hook (0:00–0:08)
- **VO:** "A poor person buys the machine first. A rich person sells the first job. Same pressure washer. Opposite order."
- **Visual:** Title vẽ tay + VERSUS + 2 nhân vật (tạp dề đỏ vs xanh) chia đôi màn hình; giữa là máy rửa xe phác thảo
- **Text:** `How Poor vs Rich People Buy Their Tools` / `VERSUS` / `POOR` (đỏ) / `RICH` (xanh)

## SCENE 2 — Poor: mua máy trước (0:08–0:30)
- **VO:** "The poor person wants to start pressure washing. Four hundred dollars for a machine. Credit card. Twenty-two percent interest. Zero customers. But now there's a pressure washer in the garage. They feel like a business owner. They didn't buy a pressure washer. They bought a feeling."
- **Visual:** nhân vật đỏ ôm máy mới tinh; thẻ tín dụng "22%" lơ lửng; bong bóng "ông chủ" vỡ tan ở câu feeling
- **Text:** `$400` / `22% APR` / `0 customers` → `"They bought a feeling."` (đỏ đậm)

## SCENE 3 — Poor: phép tính (0:30–1:12)
- **VO:** "Two jobs come in. One hundred fifty dollars each. Three hundred dollars. Then nothing. The machine sits. The payments don't. Eventually, they sell it for one hundred fifty dollars. But they keep paying the card. Spread the purchase over twelve monthly payments, and that four hundred costs about four hundred fifty. Three hundred from customers. One hundred fifty from selling the machine. Four hundred fifty in. Four hundred fifty out. Then forty dollars for fuel and supplies. Two jobs. No machine left. About forty dollars short. The only one who made money was the credit card company. They bought the equipment. They never built the demand."
- **Visual:** bảng tính hiện từng bước; máy cũ "$150", sao kê thẻ; nhân vật đỏ nhìn số âm
- **Text (lần lượt):** `2 × $150 = $300` → `sell: +$150` → `$400 → $450 (12 mo)` → `$450 in / $450 out` → `+ $40 fuel` → `= −$40` (đỏ lớn) → `"The only one who made money was the credit card company."` → `"They bought the equipment. They never built the demand."`

## SCENE 4 — Transition (1:12–1:17)
- **VO:** "Here's what a rich person does before spending that four hundred."
- **Visual:** máy rửa xe trượt sang, nhân vật xanh bước vào
- **Text:** `before spending that $400`

## SCENE 5 — Rich ①② (1:17–1:38)
- **VO:** "First, they book the work. Two driveways. Same one hundred fifty dollars each. The first purchase is a customer's decision to say yes. Second, they rent. Sixty dollars for one day, once the jobs are confirmed. They need the machine for the work — not for the garage."
- **Visual:** lịch 2 sân được đánh dấu "YES"; máy thuê "$60/day" chỉ hiện khi job confirmed
- **Text:** `① Book first` / `"The first purchase is a customer's YES."` → `② Rent $60/day` / `confirmed jobs only`

## SCENE 6 — Rich ③ (1:38–1:54)
- **VO:** "Third, they put both jobs on that rental day. Three hundred dollars in. Sixty for the machine. Forty for fuel and supplies. Two hundred left before taxes and other overhead. Same two jobs. Same prices. Different equipment bill."
- **Visual:** 2 sân dồn vào 1 ngày trên lịch; phép tính xanh hiện từng bước
- **Text:** `③ $300 − $60 − $40 = $200` / `"Same jobs. Different equipment bill."`

## SCENE 7 — Rich ④ (1:54–2:10)
- **VO:** "Fourth, with more work booked, they buy used. One hundred fifty dollars. Tested. Working. Cash. The machine the poor person just sold. Now they've got a pressure washer and fifty dollars left from those first jobs. No card balance."
- **Visual:** MŨI TÊN NỐI: máy cũ từ scene 3 (nhân vật đỏ) chuyển sang tay nhân vật xanh — "the machine the poor person just sold"
- **Text:** `④ $150 used — cash` / `"The machine the poor person just sold."` → `$200 − $150 = $50 left` / `$0 card balance` (xanh)

## SCENE 8 — Closing card (2:10–2:28)
- **VO:** "One person bought a machine and hoped customers would pay for it. The other found customers, then let them pay for the machine. Poor people buy tools to feel like a business. Rich people let the business buy the tools. Same pressure washer. Opposite first purchase."
- **Visual:** 2 nhân vật đối mặt, máy rửa xe ở giữa, 3 dòng verdict hiện lần lượt
- **Text:** `"Bought a machine, hoped customers would pay."` / `"Found customers, let them pay for the machine."` → `"Poor people buy tools to feel like a business."` → `"Rich people let the business buy the tools."` → `Same pressure washer. Opposite first purchase.`
