# Kịch bản FINAL — "How Poor vs Rich People Buy Their Tools"

*Bản lock sản xuất: kịch bản của Lucas + 2 tweak (title series trên title card; cấy lại câu "credit card company"). Thay thế bản v1 "cháy".*
*Caption: "Do you buy tools like a poor or rich person."*

## VOICEOVER (English)

**[Title card on screen: How Poor vs Rich People Buy Their Tools]**

A poor person buys the machine first.

A rich person sells the first job.

Same pressure washer. Opposite order.

The poor person wants to start pressure washing.

Four hundred dollars for a machine. Credit card. Twenty-two percent interest.

Zero customers.

But now there's a pressure washer in the garage. They feel like a business owner.

They didn't buy a pressure washer. They bought a feeling.

Two jobs come in. One hundred fifty dollars each.

Three hundred dollars.

Then nothing.

The machine sits. The payments don't.

Eventually, they sell it for one hundred fifty dollars. But they keep paying the card.

Spread the purchase over twelve monthly payments, and that four hundred costs about four hundred fifty.

Three hundred from customers. One hundred fifty from selling the machine.

Four hundred fifty in. Four hundred fifty out.

Then forty dollars for fuel and supplies.

Two jobs. No machine left. About forty dollars short.

The only one who made money was the credit card company.

They bought the equipment. They never built the demand.

Here's what a rich person does before spending that four hundred.

First, they book the work.

Two driveways. Same one hundred fifty dollars each.

The first purchase is a customer's decision to say yes.

Second, they rent.

Sixty dollars for one day, once the jobs are confirmed.

They need the machine for the work — not for the garage.

Third, they put both jobs on that rental day.

Three hundred dollars in.

Sixty for the machine. Forty for fuel and supplies.

Two hundred left before taxes and other overhead.

Same two jobs. Same prices. Different equipment bill.

Fourth, with more work booked, they buy used.

One hundred fifty dollars. Tested. Working. Cash.

The machine the poor person just sold.

Now they've got a pressure washer and fifty dollars left from those first jobs.

No card balance.

One person bought a machine and hoped customers would pay for it.

The other found customers, then let them pay for the machine.

Poor people buy tools to feel like a business.

Rich people let the business buy the tools.

Same pressure washer. Opposite first purchase.

Thứ hai, họ thuê.

Sáu mươi đô một ngày, khi job đã chắc chắn.

Thứ ba, họ dồn cả hai job vào một ngày thuê.

Cùng một cái máy rửa xe. Thứ tự mua đầu tiên ngược nhau.

## Thay đổi so với bản Lucas gửi (2 tweak)

1. **Title card:** giữ "How Poor vs Rich People Buy Their Tools" trên màn hình (đúng format series), thesis hook của Lucas làm voiceover.
2. **Cấy lại 1 câu** sau "About forty dollars short": "The only one who made money was the credit card company." — cú đâm kết cho nửa nghèo trước khi sang thesis "never built the demand".

## Kiểm toán số liệu

- $400 @ 22% APR, 12 kỳ: ~$37.51/tháng → tổng ~$450 (đã verify công thức PMT ở bản v1).
- Poor: vào $300 (jobs) + $150 (bán máy) = $450; ra $450 (máy) + $40 (xăng/vật tư) = $490 → **âm $40**. Kín.
- Rich: $300 − $60 (thuê) − $40 (xăng) = $200; mua máy cũ $150 → còn **$50**, nợ thẻ $0. Kín.
- Chi tiết "cái máy người nghèo vừa bán" ($150) khớp giá bán lại của nửa nghèo — gương hoàn hảo.
