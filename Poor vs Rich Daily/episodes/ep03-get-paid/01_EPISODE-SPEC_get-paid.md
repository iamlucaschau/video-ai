**Caption:** "Do you get paid like a poor or rich person?"

**Phong cách — lý do:** kịch bản là chuyện tin nhắn nhắc nợ, hóa đơn, khách "Seen" rồi im lặng, tiền cọc vào tài khoản (tín hiệu ●● của D: cuộc nói chuyện + tiền vào/ra tài khoản). Luân phiên: Ep01 A · Ep02 C · Ep03 D.

## SCENE 1 — Title + thesis hook (0:00–0:10)
- **VO:** "A poor person paints the house, then asks to get paid. A rich person gets paid before the first brushstroke. Same job. Opposite order."
- **Visual:** thẻ tiêu đề trong luồng chat; hai cách trả lời: POOR (đỏ) gửi hóa đơn sau khi sơn xong, RICH (xanh) gửi yêu cầu cọc trước khi bắt đầu
- **Text:** `How Poor vs Rich People Get Paid` / `INVOICE` (đỏ, sau) vs `DEPOSIT` (xanh, trước) / `Same job.` / `Opposite order.`

## SCENE 2 — Poor: nhận việc, quẹt thẻ (0:10–0:30)
- **VO:** "The poor person paints houses. Two clients book the same month. Two thousand dollars each. Paint and supplies cost six hundred a house. It goes on a credit card. Four days of work. Then they send the invoice and wait. They didn't make a sale. They made a loan."
- **Visual:** chat với khách: "Booked!" ×2; thông báo thẻ "−$600 Paint & supplies"; thẻ hóa đơn `$2,000 · due in 30 days`; câu đắt
- **Text:** `$2,000` ×2 → `−$600` (thẻ) → `INVOICE $2,000` → `"They didn't make a sale. They made a loan."`

## SCENE 3 — Poor: khoản vay (0:30–1:12) ★ TEST SCENE
- **VO:** "Here's how the loan goes. Client one pays forty-five days later. After three "just checking in" texts. The card charges twenty-four percent a year. Six hundred dollars for a month and a half. Eighteen dollars in interest. Two thousand, minus six hundred, minus eighteen. One thousand three hundred eighty-two. A month and a half late. Client two loves the walls. Then stops answering. Six hundred in paint, still on the card. Four days of work, gone. One thousand three hundred eighty-two, minus six hundred. Seven hundred eighty-two dollars for eight days of painting. And a card that's still charging. The only one who got paid on time was the card company. They sold the paint job. They never sold the terms."
- **Visual:** chat khách một: 3 tin "Just checking in…" (ngày 30, 38, 44), khách trả ngày 45; app ngân hàng: lãi thẻ `$18`, `$1,382`; chat khách hai: "Love the walls!" → tin nhắn của POOR "Seen" rồi im lặng; dòng `−$600` vẫn trên thẻ; tổng `$782`
- **Text:** `Day 45` → `24% APR → $18` → `$2,000 − $600 − $18 = $1,382` → `Seen` → `−$600` → `$1,382 − $600 = $782` (đỏ) → `"The only one who got paid on time was the card company."` → `"They never sold the terms."`

## SCENE 4 — Transition (1:12–1:16)
- **VO:** "Here's what a rich person does before the first brushstroke."
- **Visual:** cùng khách đặt lịch, lần này với RICH
- **Text:** `The rich way`

## SCENE 5 — Rich ①② (1:16–1:36)
- **VO:** "First, the price comes with terms. Half to book the date. Half at the walkthrough. It's called a deposit. Second, the deposit buys the paint. One thousand in. Six hundred on supplies. Four hundred left before day one. The card stays at zero."
- **Visual:** thẻ báo giá có điều khoản 50/50; nhãn `① Terms`, `② Deposit buys the paint`; thông báo "Deposit received $1,000"; app ngân hàng: `+$1,000` → `−$600` → `$400`; thẻ tín dụng `$0`
- **Text:** `50% to book · 50% at walkthrough` → `DEPOSIT` → `+$1,000 − $600 = $400` → `Card: $0`

## SCENE 6 — Rich ③ (1:36–1:52)
- **VO:** "Third, the walkthrough. Client one checks every wall. The second thousand lands before the tape comes off. Zero days waiting. Client two? Same walls. Same silence. But the deposit is already in. Four hundred is kept."
- **Visual:** khách một "Looks perfect!" + thông báo `+$1,000` ngay buổi nghiệm thu; **khách hai — cùng khách đã biến mất ở nửa Poor** (nút thắt): "Seen", im lặng; dòng cọc `$400` vẫn giữ
- **Text:** `③ Walkthrough` → `+$1,000 · 0 days` → `Seen` → `Deposit kept: $400`

## SCENE 7 — Rich ④ (1:52–2:05)
- **VO:** "Fourth, the math. Client one: two thousand minus six hundred. One thousand four hundred. Client two: four hundred. One thousand eight hundred dollars. Same two clients. More than double."
- **Visual:** app ngân hàng RICH: từng dòng hiện ra, tổng `$1,800` (xanh) đập vào màn hình; dòng so sánh `$782` (đỏ); `×2+`
- **Text:** `Client one: $2,000 − $600 = $1,400` → `Client two: $400` → `$1,800` → `vs $782`

## SCENE 8 — Closing card (2:05–2:20)
- **VO:** "One person did the work, then hoped to get paid. The other got paid, then did the work. Poor people send invoices. Rich people take deposits. Same job. Opposite order."
- **Visual:** thẻ tổng kết hai bên: POOR `$782` (2 hóa đơn, 1 khách biến mất, thẻ còn nợ) / RICH `$1,800` (2 khoản cọc, thẻ $0)
- **Text:** 2 dải bridge couplet → `Poor people send invoices.` (đỏ) / `Rich people take deposits.` (xanh) → `Same job. Opposite order.`
